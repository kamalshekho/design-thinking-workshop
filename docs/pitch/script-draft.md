# Product video: storyboard and capture draft

Status: proposal for review. No narration, footage or video has been produced.

## Agreed brief

The audience is the teaching team acting as the jury, with a larger audience
present. A laptop will drive a digital board with good sound. The team already
has its live presentation text. This effort covers only the following
30-second product video in HyperFrames and its audio through ElevenLabs.
The team's live presentation text is outside the scope of this effort.

The story follows one Application from the applicant form to a Staff member
making contact. The benefit to the Association takes priority over showing
every dashboard feature. Prepare the script and source material before video
production. Screen recordings supplied by the team are welcome source material.

All times below are editorial targets relative to the start of the video.
They are not measured voiceover durations. Allow the actual voice recording
to determine the final cuts. The voiceover below is an unapproved proposal
for the video only; confirm whether the team already has video narration

The last cards represent the proposed human outcome, not completed onboarding.

## Visual and sound direction

Proposed direction: generous space, large English titles, close views of the
actual German interface, and purposeful transitions that preserve the identity
of the Application. Use the same name, Category and message across shots.
Leave existing product language unchanged.

Move attention from Category to Application to Owner to contact. Give each
important action a readable hold. The emotional turn is the reply, followed by
a calmer final frame. Category maintenance is omitted from this first draft
to preserve that progression; it is not needed to explain the central benefit.

Proposed voice: warm, confident conversational English, with room for pauses.
Use a restrained music bed that builds into the dashboard reveal and resolves
at the final connection. Select the voice and sound sources before final
animation; their exact timing is still open.

## Capture material

Record separate takes at normal speed. Leave a still pause before and after
each action. Capture the highest clean resolution the machine supports,
preferably 2560 by 1440 or above, with a consistent browser viewport. Do not
add zooms, transitions, music or narration to the source recordings.

Use fictional demo details consistently across both interfaces. For example,
"Alex Weber", "alex.weber@example.com", Category "Social Media", and message
"Ich möchte eure Social-Media-Arbeit unterstützen." These are proposed
production fixtures, not facts about an actual applicant.

| Take | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| Form overview | Hold the untouched form with its heading visible. |
| Category | Open the route selector and select Social Media. |
| Application | Enter matching fictional details, complete required fields and consent, then submit; hold the success state. |
| Dashboard overview | Hold the dashboard overview and Applications list. |
| Application detail | Open the matching Application and pause on its message. |
| Ownership | Open the Owner selector, choose a Staff member and hold the result. |
| Contact | Select "E-Mail schreiben" and capture an external draft to the same applicant. |

Suggested German draft reply: "Hallo Alex, danke für dein Interesse! Lass
uns besprechen, wie du uns bei Social Media unterstützen kannst."

No actual email delivery is required for the recording. Hide unrelated tabs,
notifications and personal information. Supply original video files so the
editor can crop and retime the footage without an extra compression pass.

## Continuity and implementation boundary

Local source inspection shows separate form mock submission data and dashboard
in-memory fixtures. It does not establish a live form-to-dashboard handoff or
the state of an external deployment. Before capture, either verify a connected
environment or prepare matching demo states and describe the result as a
prototype walkthrough. Do not claim real-time integration from edited footage.

The dashboard's contact action is an external `mailto:` link, not an integrated
email composer. The reply shot must preserve this distinction. Dashboard notes
are internal and must never stand in for an outgoing reply.
