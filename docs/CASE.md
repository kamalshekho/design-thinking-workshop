## Organization

"Ich bin hier e.V." is a registered association (eingetragener Verein) that raises
awareness about online hate among three audiences:

- social media users
- media representatives
- policymakers

The association is run by a handful of staff members and volunteers.

## Current Situation

Most people who want to volunteer or ask a question contact "Ich bin hier e.V."
by email. All questions and applications arrive in one dedicated inbox.

The staff members do not have enough time to

- answer all incoming emails,
- schedule appointments with interested people,
- onboard new volunteers.

## Consequence

Applications are put on hold. New volunteers are not onboarded, so the
association stagnates and its capacity to raise awareness about online hate
stays limited.

## Problem Statement

"Ich bin hier e.V." has the problem of not having enough time, manpower and
infrastructure to handle all applications when volunteers apply online through
email.

## Solution Direction

Our solution should deliver a system that

- takes work off the employees,
- keeps the applications organized.

## Purpose — Team Motivation

**Why does this project matter?**

While countless people are willing to volunteer to make the world a better
place, employees of the foundation struggle to cope with huge amounts of
applicants leading them to stop accepting applicants due to the lack of manpower
and time. This project helps solve this problem by designing a form-like program
to filter and organize applicants for the foundation.

**What can we learn from this workshop?**

- user centric development
- working and organizing as a group
- cultural and language exchange
- exploring new technologies
- gaining more experience with the design thinking process
